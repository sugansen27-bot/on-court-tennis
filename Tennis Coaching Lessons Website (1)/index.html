Every lesson is built around you.

No two players are the same, so no two lessons look the same. Here's the arc every session moves through — from first split-step to match-ready.

01 — Warm Up

Footwork & fundamentals

Split-step timing, ready position, and quick-feet drills so you're already in position before the ball even crosses the net.

02 — Technique

Stroke & serve work

Forehand, backhand, and serve mechanics taken apart and rebuilt with repetition until they hold up under pressure.

03 — Play

Live points & strategy

Everything comes together in real rallies and scored points, with feedback right after every game — not just at the end.

Your coach

Hi, I'm your coach.

I'm Sugan, a tennis coach for the City of Eden Prairie and a player on the Eden Prairie varsity tennis team. I've been playing since age 3, and I train with the Minnetonka Tennis Club and USTA's junior program. I've played regional finals three years running and competed on the Under-14 Regional team.

I love passing on what I've learned — every lesson is patient, clear, and built around what you want to improve, whether that's your very first swing or sharpening a competitive game.
